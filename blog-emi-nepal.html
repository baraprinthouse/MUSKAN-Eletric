How EMI on an electric scooter works

A monthly payment is only one part of a finance offer. Check the amount financed, total payable and every fee before signing.

Understand the starting price

Confirm the on-road price in writing and ask which charges are included. The advertised vehicle price may not include registration, insurance or other applicable costs.

Check down payment and amount financed

The amount financed is generally the eligible purchase amount less the down payment and any adjustments. Ask the finance provider to give you a written repayment schedule.

Compare 0% and regular EMI

A 0% offer may apply only to selected models, a limited period or eligible customers. Confirm whether processing fees, documentation charges or other costs apply. For regular financing, check the interest rate, whether it is flat or reducing-balance, the tenure and any early repayment terms.

Read the agreement

Ask for the total amount payable, late-payment consequences, insurance requirements, guarantor requirements and a copy of the signed agreement.

Finance eligibility and terms are set by the finance provider. Muskan Electric must confirm the current offer before you rely on a calculator estimate.
