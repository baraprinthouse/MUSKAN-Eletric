OWNERSHIP GUIDE · DRAFT

Electric scooter maintenance: a simple checklist

Electric vehicles still need routine checks. Use the owner's manual and service schedule for the exact model you own.

Tyres and brakes

Check tyre condition and pressure at the intervals in the owner's manual. Notice changes in braking response and have unusual noises, vibration or reduced braking checked promptly.

Lights, indicators and controls

Check the headlight, tail light, indicators, horn, mirrors and display before regular use. Report warning lights or unusual behaviour to an authorised service point.

Battery and charging equipment

Use only the charger approved for your vehicle. Keep connectors clean and dry, avoid damaged cables, and follow the manufacturer's instructions for storage and charging.

Keep service records

Keep purchase documents, warranty information and service records together. Ask the dealer which scheduled inspections are required and which conditions may affect warranty coverage.

This checklist is general information, not a substitute for the vehicle's model-specific maintenance manual or warranty terms.
