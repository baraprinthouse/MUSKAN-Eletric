Charging an electric scooter at home

Before taking an EV home, understand the vehicle's charging requirements and the electrical setup where you plan to charge it.

Read the vehicle manual first

Ask the dealer for the official charging instructions for the exact model, including the charger supplied, input requirements and recommended charging practice.

Check the electrical point

Have a qualified electrician assess the socket, wiring, earthing and circuit protection where needed. Do not use damaged cables, loose adapters or improvised extensions.

Choose a suitable location

Use a dry, ventilated place away from water, heat sources and combustible materials. Keep the charger and vehicle away from children and avoid covering the charger while it is operating.

Ask about charging time

Charging time can vary by battery size, charger rating, temperature and state of charge. Use the manufacturer's stated figures for the exact model rather than a general estimate.

This is general guidance only. Follow the vehicle and charger manufacturer's manual and advice from a qualified electrician.
