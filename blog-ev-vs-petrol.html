Electric vs petrol in Nepal: what should buyers compare?

The right choice depends on how you travel, where you charge and what the full ownership cost looks like for your situation.

Start with your daily journey

Write down your usual daily distance, the longest trip you make regularly and whether you can charge at home or work. Compare those needs with the official range information and charging guidance for each vehicle you are considering.

Compare the full running cost

Use your own electricity tariff, petrol price, typical distance and the vehicle's verified efficiency. Ask the dealer for the manufacturer's official figures. Do not rely on a headline range or an estimated saving without checking the assumptions.

Ask about battery and warranty

Confirm battery chemistry, capacity, warranty duration, exclusions, service intervals and the availability of replacement parts in writing.

Test ride before you decide

Try the vehicle on roads similar to the ones you use. Check seating position, braking, carrying capacity, storage and how it feels with a passenger if relevant.

This article is general buyer education, not a claim about a specific model, subsidy, tax benefit or guaranteed saving. Confirm current specifications and local terms with the dealer and relevant authorities.
