GOOD TO KNOW

Is 0% EMI available on every vehicle?

0% EMI is offered only on selected models or campaigns, subject to eligibility, finance partner approval and written offer terms. Contact us to confirm current availability.

Can I exchange my petrol scooter or bike?

You can submit an exchange enquiry with your vehicle details and photos. Our team will review it; final valuation is confirmed after physical inspection.

Where can I visit Muskan Electric?

Our service area includes Birgunj, Parwanipur and Simara. Please call before travelling so we can confirm the exact location and availability.

How do I book a test ride?

Use the test-ride form to share your preferred model, showroom and contact details. Our team will confirm your appointment.
